# Audit Planning and Strategy

## Purpose
Develop overall audit strategy and detailed audit plan per SA 300. Direct effort to risk areas, set scope, allocate resources, define timelines, and document supervisory framework.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every statutory audit engagement, after acceptance and before fieldwork.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Limited review or compilation engagement.
- AUDIT AREAS: Strategy, plan, materiality, resource allocation, timelines, supervision.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Engagement scoped to address all material risks; resources adequate.
- Framework: SA 300, SA 315, SA 320, SA 330, SA 220, Sec 143 Companies Act 2013.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Engagement Acceptance, Understanding Entity, Risk Assessment, Materiality.
- FEEDS: All execution skills (revenue, procurement, inventory, FA, tax, completion).

## Required Inputs
- Audited prior-year financials and audit file.
- Trial balance, draft financials for current year.
- Risk assessment summary, materiality computation.
- Engagement team composition and expertise matrix.
- Client calendar, expected reporting deadlines, board meeting dates.
- Statutory deadlines (AGM, MGT-7, AOC-4).

## Optional Inputs
- Internal audit plan and reports.
- Group audit instructions (if component).
- Prior year management letter and corrective action status.
- Industry benchmarking reports.

## Knowledge
- SA 300: Strategy sets scope, timing, direction; Plan details nature/timing/extent of procedures.
- SA 220: Engagement partner retains overall responsibility; supervision of assistants mandatory.
- Sec 143(2): Auditor reports on financials per law; CARO 2020 clauses reported separately.
- SA 600: Component materiality if component auditor used; group instructions mandatory.
- SA 620: Engage expert when specialised skill needed; document competence and objectivity.
- Listed Co.: Timeline per SEBI LODR — 60 days from quarter end for limited review; 90 days for annual.

## Workflow
1. Define engagement scope — entities covered (standalone, consolidated, branches), framework (Ind AS/AS), reporting deadlines.
   - IF listed THEN confirm SEBI LODR timelines and EoM/Key Audit Matters.
2. Set materiality — overall (OM), performance (PM), specific (SM) — see Materiality Determination skill.
3. Identify significant risks (revenue, fraud, related parties, estimates) from Risk Assessment.
4. Design risk responses:
   - IF significant risk → mandatory substantive + controls testing.
   - IF routine risk → substantive analytics or sampling.
   - IF inherent low → analytical only.
5. Allocate resources by risk:
   - High-risk areas → senior staff + larger sample + extended procedures.
   - Low-risk areas → junior staff + analytics.
6. Determine expert involvement:
   - IF valuation/actuarial/IT needed → engage expert under SA 620, document competence.
7. Plan nature, timing, extent of procedures:
   - Year-end vs interim — IF controls strong → interim; ELSE → year-end substantive.
   - IF listed → mandatory year-end cut-off and confirmations.
8. Set supervisory framework — partner review points, manager reviews, on-the-job supervision.
   - IF listed/PSP → EQCR mandatory at planning, completion, report.
9. Build timeline — fieldwork dates, client deliverables, internal reviews, signing date.
   - IF consolidated → align component timelines with parent.
10. Document strategy memo and detailed plan; obtain partner sign-off.
11. Communicate plan to client — PBC list, access to systems, key contacts.
12. Revisit plan if material changes occur (M&A, fraud, scope limitation).

## Decision Points
- D1: Significant risks identified? YES → mandatory substantive + controls. NO → substantive analytics.
- D2: Controls effective? YES → interim testing, reduced substantive. NO → year-end substantive only.
- D3: Expert needed? YES → engage per SA 620. NO → proceed with team.
- D4: Listed/PSP? YES → EQCR, KAM, SEBI timeline. NO → standard timeline.
- D5: Component auditor used? YES → issue instructions per SA 600. NO → plan for full scope.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Is the scope driven by risk, or by client budget/pressure?
- Are timelines realistic given client readiness?
- Have we allocated best resources to significant risks?
- Has management's narrative on changes been independently verified?
- Are we relying on prior-year strategy without current-year risk refresh?
- Does the plan address prior-year audit issues and management letter points?

## Considerations
- Materiality changes during audit require strategy update (SA 320.12).
- Group audits: Coordinate with component auditor; obtain understanding of work.
- First-year audits: Opening balances, prior-period comparatives (SA 510).
- IT environment: Plan ITGCs and CAATs where systems material.
- Regulatory: RBI/FDIC/SEBI directions for regulated entities.
- Remote audits: Plan evidence gathering where physical access restricted.

## Common Risks
- Under-scoping significant risks due to fee pressure.
- Over-reliance on interim without reconfirming controls.
- Inadequate supervision of junior team on complex areas.
- Missing EQCR for listed engagements — SA 220 breach.
- Timeline slip causing rushed final procedures — quality risk.
- Not revisiting plan when risk profile changes mid-audit.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Management pressure to reduce scope or sample.
- Client request for early sign-off near year-end.
- Resistance to confirmations or expert involvement.
- Unusual transactions emerging after planning — update plan.
- KMP changes during the year — re-evaluate risk.
- Internal audit function weakened — override risk rises.

## Common Pitfalls
- Strategy memo copied from prior year without update.
- Not documenting risk response linkage to identified risks.
- Planning ITGCs after substantive testing starts.
- Ignoring CARO 2020 reporting requirements in scope.
- Skipping component materiality calculation.

## Validation
- [ ] Strategy memo signed by engagement partner.
- [ ] Risk responses mapped to each significant risk.
- [ ] Materiality computed and approved.
- [ ] Resource allocation aligned to risk ranking.
- [ ] Timeline meets statutory deadlines (AGM, MGT-7, AOC-4).
- [ ] EQCR sign-off for listed/PSP.
- [ ] Plan revisited if material changes occur.

## Expected Outputs
- Audit Strategy Memo (signed by partner).
- Audit Plan with risk-response matrix.
- PBC list communicated to client.
- Resource allocation and timeline schedule.
- Expert engagement letter (if applicable).
- Component auditor instructions (if applicable).

## Failure Conditions
- Risk assessment not completed before planning.
- Materiality not determined.
- Resources inadequate for identified risks.
- Statutory deadlines not feasible.

## Escalation Conditions
- Significant risk identified post-planning → STOP, update strategy, partner approval.
- Client refuses expert or component auditor → STOP, escalate, consider scope limitation.
- Timeline cannot meet SEBI/AGM deadline → STOP, escalate to Risk Management Partner.
- Fraud risk emerges → STOP, trigger SA 240 workflow, re-plan.
