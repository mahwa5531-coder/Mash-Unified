# External Confirmations

## Purpose
Design, perform, and evaluate external confirmations to obtain reliable audit evidence per SA 505.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Receivables, payables, bank balances, loans, investments held by third parties, contingent liabilities, guarantees.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Sufficient appropriate evidence from other sources already obtained (immaterial balances).
- AUDIT AREAS: Bank balances, trade receivables, trade payables, loans, contingent liabilities, related party balances.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Existence, completeness, accuracy, valuation (via supplementary procedures).
- Framework: SA 505; SA 330; SA 530; SA 700.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Risk Assessment, Revenue Testing, Borrowings Testing, Related Party Identification.
- FEEDS: Evaluation of Misstatements, Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting.

## Required Inputs
- List of balances for confirmation (debtors, creditors, banks, related parties).
- Sample selection working paper; materiality and performance materiality.
- Management authorisation letter; confirmation template per SA 505.A2-A4.
- Reconciliation of debtor / creditor ledger with control account.

## Optional Inputs
- Prior-year confirmation responses; industry response rate data; electronic platform access.

## Knowledge
- SA 505.5: Determine need for confirmation based on risk assessment and sufficiency of other evidence.
- SA 505.6: Design request — address, respondent, information requested, response mechanism.
- SA 505.7: Auditor controls mailing / dispatch; management authorisation in request.
- SA 505.8: Follow-up procedures for non-response — alternative procedures.
- SA 505.10: Reliability of response — verify source (callback, electronic authentication).

## Workflow
1. Determine accounts / balances requiring confirmation based on risk and materiality.
2. Select sample — IF high risk → 100% of material balances; IF medium → stratified sample; IF low → smaller sample; include all related party balances; zero balances and old disputed balances per judgement.
3. Design confirmation request — address to specific respondent; include specific information (balance, date, terms); authorise respondent to reply directly to auditor.
4. Obtain management authorisation letter on letterhead signed by authorised signatory; IF refusal → alternative procedures or scope limitation (SA 705).
5. Send confirmations — auditor controls mailing (auditor's stamp / courier / email from auditor); IF management insists on sending → DO NOT rely; use electronic platform for reliability.
6. Maintain dispatch and receipt register — track each confirmation (sent date, received date, response status, exceptions).
7. Follow up non-responses — send second / third reminder; IF still no response → perform alternative procedures.
8. Alternative procedures for non-response (SA 505.8) — subsequent cash receipts / payments; sales invoices; goods received notes; shipping documents; correspondence.
9. Evaluate responses — IF response agrees → corroborative evidence; IF exception → investigate; IF dispute → reconcile to ledger and assess provision.
10. Verify reliability of response — callback respondent (verify phone from independent source); IF electronic → verify platform authentication.
11. Address exception — IF timing difference → reconcile; IF genuine dispute → provision / disclosure; IF fraud indicator → SA 240.
12. Aggregate exceptions and misstatements per SA 450. Conclude on sufficiency and appropriateness of evidence obtained.

## Decision Points
- D1: Confirmation necessary (high risk or insufficient other evidence)? YES → perform. NO → alternative procedures.
- D2: Management authorises confirmation? YES → proceed. NO → alternative procedures / scope limitation.
- D3: Response received within reasonable time? YES → evaluate. NO → alternative procedures.
- D4: Response agrees with books? YES → corroborative. NO → investigate exception.
- D5: Exception represents timing difference? YES → reconcile. NO → dispute / misstatement / fraud.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Is the respondent genuinely independent or related to management?
- Does the response address match the independent source (website, prior correspondence)?
- Is the confirmation signed by authorised signatory with verifiable designation?
- Are exceptions systematically explained as timing differences without investigation?
- Is response rate unusually low for the industry?

## Considerations
- Negative confirmation acceptable only for low risk + large population + expected low error rate.
- Bank confirmations: standard format per RBI / ICAI; include all accounts, cc limits, contingent liabilities.
- Trade receivable confirmation: include terms, ageing, disputes; specify balance as at year-end.
- Confirmations from related parties — corroborate with other evidence (lower reliability alone).
- Foreign balances — coordinate with component auditor (SA 600) where applicable.

## Common Risks
- Response rate low; alternative procedures insufficient.
- Management intercepts confirmations (sent through management).
- Exceptions dismissed as timing differences without investigation.
- Related party confirmations relied upon without corroboration.
- Bank confirmations incomplete (missing accounts / contingent liabilities).

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Confirmations sent through management (interception risk).
- Responses from related parties only — no independent customers.
- Forged confirmation responses (verify letterhead, signature, address).
- Customer addresses same as company address (shell entity).
- Unusually high response rate from one cluster of customers (possible collusion).

## Common Pitfalls
- Not maintaining dispatch / receipt register.
- Accepting management-sent confirmations.
- Failing to perform alternative procedures for non-responses.
- Treating all exceptions as timing differences.
- Not verifying reliability of electronic responses.

## Validation
- [ ] Confirmation need assessed based on risk and materiality.
- [ ] Sample designed; related party balances included.
- [ ] Auditor-controlled dispatch; management authorisation obtained.
- [ ] Dispatch / receipt register maintained.
- [ ] Non-responses followed up; alternative procedures performed.

## Expected Outputs
- External confirmations master working paper with dispatch / receipt register.
- Sample selection memo (risk-based, stratified).
- Bank confirmation responses file; trade receivable / payable responses file.
- Alternative procedures memo for non-responses.

## Failure Conditions
- Management refuses to authorise confirmations.
- Response rate exceptionally low with no alternative evidence available.
- Confirmation responses appear forged / intercepted.

## Escalation Conditions
- Management intercepts confirmations / refuses authorisation → STOP, scope limitation (SA 705), partner consultation.
- Forged confirmations identified → STOP, SA 240 fraud risk, Sec 143(12) reporting.
- Material balance cannot be confirmed or verified via alternative procedures → STOP, scope limitation, modify opinion.
- Related party confirmations unreliable with no corroboration → STOP, SA 550, partner consultation.
