# Related Party Identification

## Purpose
Independently identify related parties not in management's list per SA 550 and Sec 2(76) of Companies Act.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every statutory audit — supplementary to Related Party Transactions Testing skill.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Never skip — independent identification is mandatory per SA 550.
- AUDIT AREAS: Shareholders register, board minutes, KMP disclosures, prior-year working papers, upstream/downstream flows.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Completeness of related party identification.
- Framework: SA 550; Sec 2(76) Companies Act 2013; Ind AS 24; CARO 2020 Cl. (xvii).

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Risk Assessment, Board Minutes Review, Shareholder Records Review, JE Testing.
- FEEDS: Related Party Transactions Testing, Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting.

## Required Inputs
- Shareholders' register (MGT-1); MGT-7 annual return; beneficial ownership (PMLA / Sec 89).
- Board minutes (full year + post year-end till authorization); KMP disclosures.
- Prior-year related party working papers; subsidiary/associate/JV structure (AOC-1).
- Bank statements; significant contracts; vendor / customer master.

## Optional Inputs
- Stock exchange disclosures (listed); SEBI SAST disclosures; PEP / sanctions screening; forensic reports.

## Knowledge
- SA 550.8: Auditor performs procedures to identify RPT not in management's list.
- Sec 2(76): Related party — director's relatives, KMP, firms with director/partner, subsidiary/holding/associate, JV, subsidiary of holding.
- Sec 89: Beneficial owner disclosure — register maintained by company.
- Ind AS 24.9: Disclose relationship even if no transactions.
- SA 550.A3-A5: Substance over form; principal-principal relationships (controlling shareholders).

## Workflow
1. Obtain management's related party list; tie to prior-year list and assess changes.
2. Review shareholders' register (MGT-1) and MGT-7 — identify promoters, >10% holders, beneficial owners (Sec 89); IF new shareholder entered during year → assess relationship.
3. Review board minutes (full year) — identify directors, KMP appointments, related party disclosures; conflict of interest declarations.
4. Review KMP disclosures — identify CEO/MD/CFO/CS/whole-time director; their relatives per Sec 2(77); firms/companies where they are partners/directors.
5. Review subsidiary / associate / JV structure (AOC-1) — identify all entities in group; cross-check with consolidation working paper.
6. Review prior-year working papers — identify related parties disclosed previously; verify if still related; assess if relationship ceased.
7. Review vendor / customer master — identify names matching directors / KMP / promoters / their relatives / firms; IF address / PAN / contact matches KMP / promoter → flag potential related party.
8. Review significant contracts — identify contracts with parties outside normal vendor / customer base; assess relationship.
9. Review bank statements and significant cash flows — identify unusual receipts / payments to / from non-routine parties; assess substance.
10. Identify principal-principal relationships — controlling shareholders with significant influence; assess substance over form.
11. Apply substance over form — IF transaction structured to bypass related party definition (e.g., routing through unrelated party) THEN assess true counterparty.
12. Compare identified list with management list — IF additional parties identified THEN discuss with management; obtain representation; update list. Communicate with TCWG; assess impact on RPT testing (link to RPT Testing skill).

## Decision Points
- D1: Shareholder / KMP identified not in management list? YES → add as related party; discuss with management. NO → proceed.
- D2: Vendor / customer name matches KMP / promoter? YES → flag; obtain confirmation of relationship. NO → no flag.
- D3: Address / PAN / contact overlaps with KMP / promoter? YES → potential related party; investigate. NO → no flag.
- D4: Transaction structured to bypass related party definition? YES → substance over form; reclassify. NO → proceed.
- D5: Prior-year related party relationship ceased? YES → remove from list with documentation. NO → continue.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are shareholders acting in concert (SAST) without disclosure?
- Are vendors with similar names to KMP genuinely independent?
- Are beneficial owners (Sec 89) fully disclosed?
- Are offshore entities linked to promoters via nominee structures?
- Are directors' conflict-of-interest declarations complete?

## Considerations
- Sec 2(77): Relative definition — members of HUF, spouse, children, siblings, parents.
- SAST (SEBI Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers): persons acting in concert — related party.
- PMLA beneficial ownership: 25% ownership / control threshold.
- Substance over form: Sec 188 applies to substance, not form (route through intermediary).
- Principal-principal: controlling shareholders with material influence — disclose under Ind AS 24.

## Common Risks
- Undisclosed beneficial owners (Sec 89) omitted from list.
- Vendors / customers related to KMP / promoters not in list.
- Offshore nominee structures conceal related party status.
- Directors' conflict-of-interest declarations incomplete.
- Prior-year related parties not updated for ceased / new relationships.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Shell entities with shared addresses / PANs with promoters.
- Round-tripping through undisclosed related parties.
- Vendors created at year-end matching KMP relative names.
- Offshore nominee structures to disguise promoter control.
- Conflict-of-interest declarations suppressed in board minutes.

## Common Pitfalls
- Accepting management list without independent verification.
- Not reviewing shareholders' register (MGT-1) and beneficial ownership (Sec 89).
- Not matching vendor / customer master with KMP / promoter names.
- Not reviewing prior-year working papers for continuity.
- Not applying substance over form to routing structures.

## Validation
- [ ] Management list obtained and tied to prior year.
- [ ] Shareholders' register (MGT-1) and MGT-7 reviewed; beneficial owners identified.
- [ ] Board minutes reviewed for directors, KMP, conflict-of-interest declarations.
- [ ] Subsidiary / associate / JV structure (AOC-1) reviewed.
- [ ] Vendor / customer master screened for matches with KMP / promoter.

## Expected Outputs
- Related party identification working paper (independent list).
- Shareholder / KMP screening memo.
- Vendor / customer master screening memo (matches / overlaps).
- Beneficial owner (Sec 89) memo.

## Failure Conditions
- Shareholders' register / MGT-7 not produced.
- Board minutes incomplete / not produced.
- Management refuses to disclose beneficial owners (Sec 89).

## Escalation Conditions
- Material undisclosed related party identified → STOP, SA 550 + SA 240, partner consultation.
- Beneficial owner disclosure (Sec 89) refused → STOP, scope limitation, partner consultation.
- Offshore nominee structures concealing promoter control → STOP, coordinate with component auditor (SA 600), partner consultation.
- Pattern of routing transactions through shell entities to bypass related party definition → STOP, SA 240 fraud risk, Sec 143(12) reporting.
