# Management Representation Letter

## Purpose
Obtain written representations from management to corroborate audit evidence per SA 580.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every statutory audit — mandatory before audit report issuance.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Never skip — representation letter is mandatory per SA 580.
- AUDIT AREAS: Management responsibility, completeness, fraud, related parties, subsequent events, going concern, plans/intentions.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Corroboration of all assertions through management representations.
- Framework: SA 580; SA 560; SA 570; SA 240; SA 550.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: All audit procedures substantially complete; Subsequent Events Review; Going Concern Assessment.
- FEEDS: Audit Conclusion, Audit Report Issuance.

## Required Inputs
- Draft representation letter (signed by management / TCWG).
- Management's going concern assessment memo; subsequent events memo.
- List of related parties; fraud awareness communications; prior-year representation letter.

## Optional Inputs
- Board minutes authorising signatory; specific matter representations for unique risks.

## Knowledge
- SA 580.5: Obtain written representations from management / TCWG on matters material to FS.
- SA 580.10: Mandatory representations — management responsibility; completeness of information; no fraud; related parties; subsequent events; going concern; plans/intentions; estimates; compliance with laws.
- SA 580.11: Date representation letter no earlier than date of audit report.
- SA 580.13: If management refuses → scope limitation; possible disclaimer of opinion (SA 705); consider withdrawal.
- SA 580.16: Representations cannot replace other audit evidence; corroborate, not substitute.

## Workflow
1. Draft representation letter covering mandatory items — management responsibility for FS, internal financial controls, compliance with laws; completeness and accuracy of information provided; no fraud (management / employee); awareness of allegations; related parties complete per Sec 2(76) and Ind AS 24; subsequent events post year-end till authorization; going concern assessment, plans, material uncertainty; plans / intentions affecting carrying value; estimates assumptions reasonable; compliance with laws and regulations (Sec 143(12)).
2. Obtain specific representations for unique risks — IF litigation THEN representation on provision adequacy; IF guarantee THEN representation on probable invocation; IF related party THEN representation on completeness.
3. Date the letter — date no earlier than audit report date; align with authorization date.
4. Identify signatories — sign by CEO / MD and CFO (or those charged with governance per Sec 134(5)); IF group audit THEN obtain from group management + component management (if relevant).
5. Verify alignment with audit findings — IF representation contradicts audit evidence THEN challenge; obtain additional evidence.
6. Communicate to management — discuss each representation; obtain acknowledgement of responsibility.
7. Obtain signed letter on letterhead; signed and dated; verify authority of signatory.
8. Evaluate sufficiency — IF letter not provided / refused THEN scope limitation (SA 705); consider withdrawal (Sec 144).
9. Use representations to corroborate evidence — do not substitute representations for necessary audit procedures (SA 580.16).
10. Address group audit-specific representations — group management representation on consolidation; component management where relevant.
11. Address changes / new information post signing — IF information emerges between signing and audit report date THEN reassess; obtain updated representation.
12. File representation letter in audit documentation (SA 230); reference in audit report.

## Decision Points
- D1: Management signs representation letter covering all mandatory items? YES → proceed. NO → scope limitation; consider withdrawal.
- D2: Representation contradicts audit evidence? YES → challenge; obtain additional evidence; reassess. NO → corroborate.
- D3: Letter dated no earlier than audit report date? YES → valid. NO → obtain re-dated letter.
- D4: Group audit — separate component representation needed? YES → obtain from component management. NO → group management only.
- D5: Management refuses to provide representation? YES → scope limitation; possible disclaimer / withdrawal (Sec 144). NO → proceed.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are representations generic or specific to identified risks?
- Do representations contradict audit evidence in any area?
- Is signatory authorised (board resolution) and not coerced?
- Are specific representations on litigation / guarantees / related parties / going concern obtained?
- Are representations obtained too early (before audit completion)?

## Considerations
- Mandatory representations cover: responsibility, completeness, fraud, related parties, subsequent events, going concern, plans/intentions, estimates, laws/regulations.
- Date: no earlier than audit report date; align with authorization date (SA 560 / SA 580.11).
- Signatories: typically CEO / MD and CFO; in some cases audit committee chair.
- SA 580.16: Representations corroborate, do not substitute other evidence.
- Group audit: representation from group management on consolidation + component where relevant (SA 600).

## Common Risks
- Generic representation letter without specific representations for identified risks.
- Letter dated before audit report (invalid).
- Signatory not authorised (no board resolution).
- Representation contradicts audit evidence but accepted.
- Group audit — component management representation omitted.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Management reluctant to sign representation on fraud awareness.
- Representation contradicts whistle-blower / regulator correspondence.
- Signatory coerced / not genuinely authorised.
- Representation on going concern contradicted by adverse post year-end events.
- Representation on related parties contradicted by identified undisclosed RPT.

## Common Pitfalls
- Using generic representation template without tailoring to specific risks.
- Dating letter before audit report date.
- Not verifying signatory authority (board resolution).
- Accepting representation that contradicts audit evidence.
- Missing specific representations on litigation / related parties / going concern.

## Validation
- [ ] Representation letter covers all mandatory items (SA 580.10).
- [ ] Specific representations obtained for unique risks (litigation, guarantees, RPT, going concern).
- [ ] Letter dated no earlier than audit report date.
- [ ] Signatories authorised (board resolution) and signed.
- [ ] Representations corroborate, do not contradict, audit evidence.

## Expected Outputs
- Signed management representation letter on company letterhead.
- Signatory authority verification memo (board resolution).
- Specific representations appendix for identified risks.
- Group / component management representation letters (if applicable).

## Failure Conditions
- Management refuses to sign representation letter.
- Signatory not authorised / no board resolution.
- Letter dated before audit report and management refuses re-dating.

## Escalation Conditions
- Management refuses to provide representation → STOP, scope limitation / disclaimer (SA 705), consider withdrawal (Sec 144), partner consultation.
- Representation contradicts material audit evidence → STOP, reassess; obtain additional evidence; potential fraud (SA 240), partner consultation.
- Group audit — component management refuses representation → STOP, scope limitation (SA 705), partner consultation.
- Management integrity concerns (reluctance, coercion) → STOP, Sec 144 withdrawal consideration, partner consultation.
