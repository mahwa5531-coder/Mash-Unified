# Evaluation of Misstatements

## Purpose
Accumulate, evaluate, and communicate misstatements; assess impact on audit opinion per SA 450.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Throughout audit and at completion — every audit.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Never skip — misstatement evaluation mandatory before opinion.
- AUDIT AREAS: Factual, projected, below-threshold misstatements; aggregation; performance materiality; opinion impact.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: All assertions impacted by identified misstatements.
- Framework: SA 450; SA 320; SA 700/705; SA 230.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: All substantive testing skills; Risk Assessment; Materiality Determination.
- FEEDS: Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting, Management Representation Letter.

## Required Inputs
- Misstatement summary (factual, projected, below threshold); area-wise exception logs.
- Materiality and performance materiality; prior-year uncorrected misstatements.
- Management's response on misstatements; written representations.
- Adjusted vs unadjusted trial balance.

## Optional Inputs
- ICAI guidance on misstatement aggregation; industry-specific benchmarks.

## Knowledge
- SA 450.5: Accumulate misstatements identified during audit (factual + projected).
- SA 450.A2: Factual — no doubt about amount; projected — best estimate from sample; below threshold (clearly trivial).
- SA 450.6: Communicate accumulated misstatements to management; request correction.
- SA 450.7: Keep management informed during audit; not just at end.
- SA 450.10: If management refuses to correct AND misstatements material → qualified/adverse opinion (SA 705).

## Workflow
1. Maintain misstatement summary throughout audit — log each misstatement (amount, account, assertion, source, area).
2. Classify misstatements — Factual: definite amount; Projected: best estimate from sample per SA 530; Below threshold: clearly trivial (<1% of performance materiality).
3. Aggregate uncorrected misstatements — sum across accounts / assertions; include prior-year uncorrected carry-forward.
4. Compare to performance materiality — IF aggregate > performance materiality THEN reassess risk; extend procedures; request correction.
5. Evaluate qualitative factors — IF misstatement affects compliance (loan covenant, regulatory ratio, Sec 186 limit) THEN material regardless of size; IF turns profit into loss THEN material; IF affects KMP compensation / EPS THEN material; IF related party THEN qualitative materiality.
6. Communicate to management — provide written summary; request correction; obtain response.
7. If management corrects — verify adjustment in books; re-test adjusted balances.
8. If management refuses correction — evaluate materiality (individual and aggregate); assess impact on opinion (SA 705).
9. Request written representation — management representation on uncorrected misstatements and their belief of immateriality (SA 580).
10. Re-assess if cumulative approaches performance materiality (SA 450.11) — reassess risk; reconsider sufficiency of procedures; extend if needed.
11. Consider projection of sample misstatements (SA 530) — IF anomalous THEN isolate; IF systematic THEN project to population.
12. Document conclusion — aggregate vs materiality; qualitative factors; management response; opinion impact. Communicate to TCWG per SA 260; conclude on audit opinion (link to Audit Reporting skill).

## Decision Points
- D1: Misstatement clearly trivial (<1% performance materiality)? YES → do not accumulate separately. NO → accumulate.
- D2: Aggregate uncorrected misstatements > performance materiality? YES → reassess risk; extend procedures; request correction. NO → proceed.
- D3: Management corrects misstatements? YES → re-test adjusted balances. NO → evaluate opinion impact.
- D4: Misstatement qualitative (compliance / KMP / EPS / related party)? YES → material regardless of size. NO → quantitative test.
- D5: Aggregate material and uncorrected? YES → qualified/adverse opinion (SA 705). NO → unmodified opinion with documentation.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are misstatements systematically dismissed as "clearly trivial"?
- Are projected misstatements excluded from aggregate without basis?
- Does management consistently refuse corrections with weak justification?
- Are qualitative factors (regulatory, KMP) ignored in materiality assessment?
- Is prior-year carry-forward of uncorrected misstatements included in aggregate?

## Considerations
- Performance materiality: set below overall materiality to reduce aggregation risk (typically 50-75% of materiality).
- Below threshold (clearly trivial): typically <1% of performance materiality — not accumulated individually but tracked.
- Qualitative materiality: regulatory compliance, KMP compensation, EPS, segment performance, related party, going concern.
- Prior-year uncorrected misstatements carry forward; reassess each year.
- Projection per SA 530: project sample misstatements to population; isolate anomalous items.

## Common Risks
- Below-threshold misstatements not tracked; aggregate accumulates silently.
- Projected misstatements excluded without basis.
- Qualitative factors ignored in materiality assessment.
- Prior-year carry-forward omitted.
- Management refusals accepted without opinion modification.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Consistent pattern of misstatements just below performance materiality.
- Management consistently dismisses misstatements as "clearly trivial".
- Misstatements in related party balances treated as immaterial.
- KMP compensation misstatements suppressed.
- Sample misstatements declared anomalous without investigation.

## Common Pitfalls
- Not maintaining misstatement summary throughout audit.
- Excluding projected misstatements from aggregate.
- Ignoring qualitative materiality factors.
- Missing prior-year carry-forward.
- Not requesting management representation on uncorrected misstatements.

## Validation
- [ ] Misstatement summary maintained throughout audit.
- [ ] Misstatements classified (factual / projected / below threshold).
- [ ] Aggregate compared to performance materiality; qualitative factors assessed.
- [ ] Management communicated; correction requested; response documented.
- [ ] Prior-year carry-forward included; sample projection per SA 530.

## Expected Outputs
- Misstatement summary working paper (factual / projected / below threshold).
- Aggregate vs materiality evaluation memo.
- Qualitative factors assessment memo.
- Management communication and response memo.

## Failure Conditions
- Management refuses to provide written representation on uncorrected misstatements.
- Misstatement summary incomplete / not maintained.
- Sample documentation insufficient for projection.

## Escalation Conditions
- Aggregate uncorrected misstatements material and management refuses correction → STOP, qualified/adverse opinion (SA 705), partner consultation.
- Qualitative misstatement material (compliance / KMP / regulatory) → STOP, partner consultation.
- Misstatement pattern indicates fraud (just below materiality, cookie jar) → STOP, SA 240, Sec 143(12) reporting.
- Management representation refused → STOP, scope limitation, partner consultation (SA 580).
