# Engagement Quality Review

## Purpose
Perform independent engagement quality review before audit report issuance per SA 220.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Listed entity audits; public interest entities; high-risk engagements per firm policy.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Non-listed low-risk engagements (per firm policy; EQCR may not be mandatory).
- AUDIT AREAS: Independent partner review of significant judgments, risk assessment, conclusions, documentation.

## Audit Objective
- Deliverable: Independent EQCR sign-off before audit report issuance.
- Framework: SA 220; SA 600 (group audits); Sec 143; ICAI SQC 1 / SQM 1 & 2.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Audit substantially complete; draft audit report; engagement partner conclusions.
- FEEDS: Audit Report Issuance, Firm Quality Monitoring.

## Required Inputs
- Draft audit report and financial statements.
- Significant judgments memo; risk assessment; materiality; going concern; fraud.
- Audit file (permanent + current); review notes log; management representations.
- Engagement partner conclusions; TCWG communication memo (SA 260).

## Optional Inputs
- Prior-year EQCR review notes; industry / regulator developments; firm methodology manual.

## Knowledge
- SA 220.19: Engagement quality review — for listed entities and other engagements per firm policy.
- SA 220.20: EQCR objectives — significant judgments reviewed; conclusions reached; report appropriate.
- SA 220.21: EQCR eligibility — partner / equivalent; not on engagement team; objective; appropriate competence.
- SA 220.22: EQCR procedures — discussion with engagement partner; review of FS and report; selected working papers; significant judgments; risk assessment; conclusions.
- SA 220.23: Completion of EQCR before report release; document date of completion.

## Workflow
1. Confirm EQCR applicability — listed entity / public interest entity / firm policy → EQCR mandatory; identify EQCR partner.
2. Verify EQCR partner eligibility — not on engagement team; objective; appropriate competence; sufficient authority.
3. Provide EQCR partner access to — draft audit report; financial statements; significant judgments memo; risk assessment; selected working papers.
4. EQCR partner discussion with engagement partner — discuss significant judgments, risk assessment, conclusions, disagreements, difficult matters.
5. EQCR partner reviews significant judgments — materiality; risk assessment; going concern; estimates (SA 540); fraud (SA 240); related parties (SA 550); subsequent events (SA 560).
6. EQCR partner reviews risk assessment and response — verify risk identification, assessment, and response design; assess sufficiency.
7. EQCR partner reviews conclusions and report — verify opinion type; basis for modification; EoM / OM; KAM (listed); CARO reporting; Sec 143(3) matters.
8. EQCR partner reviews documentation — verify SA 230 compliance; significant judgments documented; review notes cleared; file assembled.
9. EQCR partner addresses group audit (if applicable) — review significant component conclusions; group materiality; component auditor evaluation (SA 600).
10. EQCR partner addresses disagreements (if any) — IF engagement team and EQCR disagree THEN discuss; document; if unresolved → report not released (SA 220.24).
11. EQCR partner documents conclusions — document review performed; matters discussed; conclusions reached; date of completion (before report release).
12. Engagement partner releases report after EQCR completion — verify EQCR completion before signing audit report. File EQCR documentation in audit file (SA 230).

## Decision Points
- D1: EQCR mandatory (listed / PIE / firm policy)? YES → engage EQCR partner. NO → document exemption.
- D2: EQCR partner eligible (not on team, objective, competent)? YES → proceed. NO → identify alternative.
- D3: Significant judgments supported by evidence? YES → proceed. NO → request additional procedures.
- D4: Opinion type appropriate? YES → proceed. NO → request reconsideration.
- D5: EQCR objections resolved? YES → release report. NO → report not released (SA 220.24).

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Is EQCR partner genuinely independent of engagement team?
- Are significant judgments genuinely challenged, or rubber-stamped?
- Are disagreements documented honestly, or sanitised?
- Are KAMs (listed) selected from significant risks, or generic?
- Is risk assessment aligned with response, or generic?

## Considerations
- EQCR is hot review — completed before audit report release.
- EQCR partner not on engagement team; not involved in day-to-day decisions.
- SA 220.24: If objections unresolved → report not released until resolved; if impossible → consider resignation.
- Group audit: EQCR covers group engagement team + significant component conclusions.
- Cold review (post-issuance): part of firm monitoring (SQM 1); not a substitute for EQCR.

## Common Risks
- EQCR partner not genuinely independent (consulting role in prior year).
- Significant judgments rubber-stamped without challenge.
- Disagreements sanitised in documentation.
- EQCR completion after report release (not before).
- Group component conclusions not reviewed.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- EQCR partner pressured to sign off without genuine review.
- Disagreements on fraud indicators suppressed.
- EQCR completion backdated before report release.
- Significant judgments on fraud (Sec 143(12)) not challenged.
- Going concern conclusion accepted despite adverse post year-end events.

## Common Pitfalls
- EQCR partner with prior consulting role (not independent).
- Significant judgments reviewed superficially.
- Disagreements not documented honestly.
- EQCR completion after report release.
- Group component conclusions not reviewed in EQCR.

## Validation
- [ ] EQCR applicability confirmed; eligible EQCR partner engaged.
- [ ] EQCR partner discussion with engagement partner documented.
- [ ] Significant judgments reviewed (materiality, risk, going concern, estimates, fraud, related parties, subsequent events).
- [ ] Risk assessment and response verified; opinion type and report reviewed.
- [ ] Documentation (SA 230) verified; review notes cleared; group component conclusions reviewed (if applicable).

## Expected Outputs
- EQCR completion memorandum documenting review performed, matters discussed, conclusions.
- Significant judgments review memo (by EQCR partner).
- Risk assessment and response review memo (by EQCR partner).
- Report review memo (opinion type, EoM/OM, KAM, CARO).

## Failure Conditions
- Eligible EQCR partner not available.
- EQCR partner not provided access to significant working papers.
- Engagement team unwilling to share disagreements.

## Escalation Conditions
- EQCR objections unresolved → STOP, report not released (SA 220.24), partner / firm consultation.
- EQCR partner not independent / eligible → STOP, engage alternative EQCR partner.
- Significant judgments not supported by evidence → STOP, request additional procedures before EQCR completion.
- Disagreement on fraud indicator (Sec 143(12)) → STOP, partner consultation, Form ADT-4 if material fraud.
- Going concern conclusion contradicted by adverse events → STOP, partner consultation, modify opinion (SA 705).
