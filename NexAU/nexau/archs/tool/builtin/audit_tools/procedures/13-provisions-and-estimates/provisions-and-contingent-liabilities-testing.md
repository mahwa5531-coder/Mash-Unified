# Provisions and Contingent Liabilities Testing

## Purpose
Test recognition, measurement, and disclosure of provisions, contingent liabilities, and contingent assets per Ind AS 37.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Warranty, restructuring, onerous contracts, litigation, tax demands, guarantees, environmental obligations.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Ind AS 19 employee benefits; Ind AS 12 income tax (use Deferred Tax Testing).
- AUDIT AREAS: Provisions, contingent liabilities, contingent assets, commitments.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Completeness, valuation, existence, presentation/disclosure.
- Framework: Ind AS 37; SA 540; SA 580; CARO 2020 Cl. (xxii)/(xxv).

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Journal Entry Testing, Legal Expense Review, Tax Testing, Board Minutes Review.
- FEEDS: Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting, Subsequent Events Review.

## Required Inputs
- Schedule of provisions and contingencies with movement workings.
- Legal case register; advocate letters; tax demands; guarantee register.
- Warranty claim history; restructuring plan board approval; onerous contract list.
- Management estimate memo with assumptions and discount rates.

## Optional Inputs
- Prior-year legal provisions file; insurance claims; environmental assessments.

## Knowledge
- Ind AS 37.14: Recognise provision if present obligation + probable outflow (>50%) + reliable estimate.
- Ind AS 37.23: Best estimate of expenditure to settle at reporting date.
- Ind AS 37.27: Discount with pre-tax rate if time value material; reflect risks specific to liability.
- Ind AS 37.10: Contingent liability — possible obligation / not probable / no estimate — disclose only.
- Ind AS 37.31: Contingent asset — recognise only when virtually certain.

## Workflow
1. Obtain complete schedule of provisions and contingencies; tie to TB and prior-year closing.
2. Test present obligation — IF legal/constructive obligation at reporting date THEN test recognition; IF only possible THEN disclose as contingent.
3. Test probable outflow (>50%) via advocate letter — IF outflow <50% THEN disclose; IF virtually certain no outflow THEN remove.
4. Test reliable estimate — recompute warranty using claim history; IF range THEN expected value (large population) or most likely (single obligation).
5. Test legal case provisions — map each case to advocate opinion; IF opinion changed post year-end THEN treat as adjusting event (SA 560).
6. Test tax demand provisions — IF disputed at first appeal with favorable precedent THEN contingent; IF unfavorable THEN assess per facts.
7. Test restructuring — verify formal plan + board approval + communication BEFORE reporting date; IF not communicated THEN no provision.
8. Test onerous contracts — identify unavoidable cost > expected benefit; IF future avoidable THEN no provision.
9. Test warranty — recompute using claim history; IF new product/period THEN validate assumption; IF actual claims exceed estimate THEN reassess.
10. Test guarantees — IF primary obligor and probable invocation THEN provision; IF remote THEN disclose only.
11. Test discounting — IF settlement >1 year and material THEN discount at pre-tax rate; reclassify unwinding to finance cost.
12. Test disclosure per Schedule III / Ind AS 37.85; contingent asset disclosed only if inflow probable. Aggregate uncorrected misstatements per SA 450.

## Decision Points
- D1: Present obligation at reporting date? YES → recognise. NO → contingent disclosure / remove.
- D2: Outflow probable (>50%)? YES → recognise. NO → disclose contingent.
- D3: Reliable estimate available? YES → measure. NO → disclose contingent.
- D4: Time value material (>1 year, large)? YES → discount. NO → nominal value.
- D5: Restructuring communicated before reporting date? YES → recognise. NO → no provision.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are legal opinions from independent advocates or in-house counsel with bias?
- Are warranty claim rates based on verifiable history or management optimism?
- Have past provisions been systematically under-accrued with later top-ups?
- Are tax demands consistently treated as contingent despite losing at first appeal?
- Are restructuring costs genuinely restructuring or operating costs in disguise?

## Considerations
- Constructive obligation arises from valid expectation created by past practice/announcement.
- Discount rate pre-tax, risk-adjusted, specific to liability (not entity).
- Reimbursement asset recognised separately only when virtually certain (Ind AS 37.47).
- Contingent liabilities reviewed every reporting date; reverse if outflow no longer probable.
- Expected value for large population; most likely amount for single obligation.

## Common Risks
- Legal provisions under-accrued based on optimistic advocate opinions.
- Warranty provision not updated for product quality deterioration.
- Restructuring provision used to smooth future operating costs.
- Contingent liabilities omitted from disclosure schedule.
- Tax demands wrongly disclosed as contingent despite high outflow probability.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Consistent under-provision of known legal claims with later top-ups.
- Advocates changed repeatedly until favorable opinion obtained.
- Guarantee to related party undisclosed and unprovisioned.
- Contingent assets disclosed prominently while contingent liabilities buried.
- Restructuring provision reversed into income next year without execution.

## Common Pitfalls
- Not sending advocate letter directly; relying on management-provided opinion.
- Treating all tax demands as contingent without merits assessment.
- Not testing discount rate independence and risk-adjustment.
- Missing constructive obligations from past announcements in board minutes.
- Not reconciling opening to closing per Ind AS 37.84 movement.

## Validation
- [ ] Complete schedule obtained; opening reconciled to prior year.
- [ ] Each material provision tested for obligation, probable outflow, reliable estimate.
- [ ] Advocate letters sent on auditor letterhead; replies evaluated.
- [ ] Warranty recomputed; restructuring plan + communication verified.
- [ ] Discounting tested; unwinding reclassified to finance cost.

## Expected Outputs
- Provisions and contingencies testing working paper with movement schedule.
- Legal case provision memo (case-wise: amount, stage, opinion).
- Warranty / restructuring / onerous contract memos.
- Advocate letter replies file.

## Failure Conditions
- Advocate letters not responded; management unwilling to send.
- Legal case register incomplete or not maintained.
- Management unable to provide basis for estimate (no reliable estimate).

## Escalation Conditions
- Advocate opinion contradicted by court order post year-end → STOP, SA 560 adjusting event, partner consultation.
- Material provision under-stated with management refusal to adjust → STOP, qualified/adverse risk.
- Undisclosed related party guarantee material → STOP, SA 550, partner consultation.
- Pattern of consistent under-provision indicating fraud → STOP, SA 240, Sec 143(12) reporting.
