# Fixed Assets Disposal Testing

## Purpose
Verify gain/loss on disposal, related-party sales, write-offs, impairment (Ind AS 36), fully depreciated assets still in use, physical vs books reconciliation.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Material disposals/write-offs; related-party transfers; impairment indicators.
- DO NOT WHEN: Disposals immaterial; no write-offs.
- AUDIT AREAS: Tangible FA, intangibles, CWIP write-offs, sale to related parties, scrapped assets.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Existence, completeness, valuation, accuracy, presentation, cut-off.
- Framework: Ind AS 16, Ind AS 36, Ind AS 24, Companies Act Sec 180/188, CARO 2020.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Fixed Assets Additions Testing, Depreciation Testing, Related Party Transactions.
- FEEDS: Audit Reporting, Evaluation of Misstatements, Deferred Tax Testing.

## Required Inputs
- Disposals schedule with date, cost, accumulated depreciation, sale proceeds, gain/loss.
- Board approvals for disposals; related-party transaction details.
- Sale agreements, auction records, scrap dealer invoices.
- Impairment assessment workings (Ind AS 36).
- Physical verification report vs FAR.

## Optional Inputs
- Independent valuation for major disposals; insurance claim settlements.

## Knowledge
- Ind AS 16.67: Derecognition at disposal — gain/loss = proceeds − carrying amount − disposal cost.
- Ind AS 16.74: Revaluation surplus on disposal → transfer directly to retained earnings (not P&L).
- Ind AS 36.12: Impairment indicators — market decline, obsolescence, idle, performance below expectation.
- Ind AS 36.55: Impairment loss reversed (except goodwill) up to original carrying amount less depreciation.
- Companies Act Sec 180(1)(a): Board approval for disposal of substantial undertaking.
- Companies Act Sec 188: Related-party disposal requires approval, disclosure.
- CARO 2020 Cl 11: Impairment loss disclosed; physical verification reconciliation.

## Workflow
1. Obtain disposals schedule — tie total to GL, P&L gain/loss, FS notes.
2. Vouch sample:
   - Sale agreement; proceeds realized in bank.
   - IF related party → arm's length pricing; Sec 188 approval, disclosure.
3. Verify gain/loss computation:
   - Gain/loss = proceeds − (cost − accumulated depreciation) − disposal cost.
   - IF revalued asset → revaluation reserve transfer to retained earnings, not P&L.
4. Verify Board approval:
   - IF substantial undertaking disposal → Sec 180 special resolution.
5. Test write-offs:
   - IF asset written off → verify authorization, evidence (scrap, obsolescence).
   - IF related-party write-off → arm's length scrutiny.
6. Test impairment (Ind AS 36):
   - Identify impairment indicators (idle, obsolete, market decline).
   - IF indicators → verify CGU identification, recoverable amount (higher of FVLCD and VIU).
   - IF impairment loss → verify P&L charge; reverse revaluation first (revalued assets).
7. Test impairment reversal:
   - IF reversal (except goodwill) → limited to carrying amount had no impairment occurred.
8. Verify fully depreciated assets still in use:
   - IF NRV/cost still relevant → retain in FAR; physical verification.
9. Reconcile physical vs books:
   - IF physical asset missing in books → verify; if genuinely unrecorded, raise exception.
   - IF book asset not physical → investigate (misappropriation, scrapping without entry).
10. Test cut-off — disposal in correct period; proceeds in same period.
11. Verify CARO 2020 disclosures: physical verification reconciliation, impairment loss.
12. Evaluate exceptions — factual/projected/anomalous; project per SA 530.

## Decision Points
- D1: Related-party disposal? YES → Sec 188 approval, arm's length. NO → proceed.
- D2: Revalued asset disposed? YES → revaluation surplus to retained earnings. NO → P&L gain/loss.
- D3: Impairment indicator present? YES → recoverable amount test. NO → no impairment.
- D4: Impairment reversal? YES → limited (not goodwill). NO → proceed.
- D5: Fully depreciated asset in use? YES → retain in FAR. NO → derecognise.
- D6: Physical vs books unreconciled? YES → investigate, adjust. NO → proceed.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are related-party disposals at arm's length or transfer of value?
- Are write-offs genuinely assets lost, or unrecorded sales?
- Is impairment timing manipulated to manage profit?
- Are fully depreciated assets still generating revenue?
- Is physical-vs-books discrepancy investigated or ignored?
- Are sale proceeds genuinely realized, or routed through related party?

## Considerations
- Tax: capital gains (IT Act); GST on disposal.
- Insurance claims: treated appropriately on settlement.
- Subsidiary stake disposal: Ind AS 27/28 separate treatment.
- Grant reversal (Ind AS 20) on disposal of grant-funded asset.

## Common Risks
- Gain/loss miscomputed; revaluation surplus routed through P&L.
- Related-party disposal without Sec 188 approval.
- Impairment not recognised despite clear indicators.
- Fully depreciated assets derecognised but still in use (or vice versa).
- Physical-vs-books reconciliation not performed or accepted without investigation.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Related-party disposal significantly below book value.
- Write-off of large asset with vague evidence.
- Impairment reversal without genuine circumstance change.
- Disposal to related party routed via shell entity.
- Manual JEs adjusting gain/loss near year-end.
- Physical-vs-books gap uninvestigated despite materiality.
- Insurance claim proceeds not recorded.

## Common Pitfalls
- Not testing revaluation reserve transfer on disposal.
- Missing Sec 180/188 approvals for related-party/substantial disposal.
- Accepting impairment computation without VIU/FVLCD review.
- Treating physical-vs-books gap as routine.

## Failure Conditions
- Disposals schedule incomplete; proceeds evidence missing.
- Impairment workings unavailable despite indicators.
- Physical-vs-books reconciliation not performed.

## Escalation Conditions
- Related-party disposal materially below book value → STOP, refer Sec 188, partner consultation.
- Impairment loss not recognised despite clear indicators → STOP, propose adjustment.
- Physical-vs-books gap material and unexplained → STOP, fraud risk assessment (SA 240), escalate.
- Revaluation surplus routed through P&L materially → STOP, propose adjustment.
