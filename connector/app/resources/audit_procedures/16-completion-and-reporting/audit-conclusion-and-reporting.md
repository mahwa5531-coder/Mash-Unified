# Audit Conclusion and Reporting

## Purpose
Form audit opinion, draft audit report, and issue report per SA 700/705/706/710/720.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every statutory audit — mandatory audit report issuance.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Review / limited assurance engagement (use SRE 2400); agreed-upon procedures.
- AUDIT AREAS: Unmodified opinion, modified opinion (qualified/adverse/disclaimer), EoM, OM, KAM, comparatives, other information, signing/dating, joint auditors.

## Audit Objective
- Deliverable: Independent auditor's report per Companies Act Sec 143 and SAs.
- Framework: SA 700, SA 705, SA 706, SA 710, SA 720, SA 260, Sec 143/134.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: All substantive testing, Evaluation of Misstatements, Going Concern Assessment, Subsequent Events Review, Management Representation Letter.
- FEEDS: Filing with MCA (AOC-4), shareholders (AGM), regulators.

## Required Inputs
- Final adjusted financial statements (BS, P&L, Cash Flow, Notes, Schedule III).
- Completed audit working papers; misstatement summary; going concern conclusion.
- CARO 2020 reporting memo; management representation letter.
- Draft audit report; prior-year audit report; comparative information.

## Optional Inputs
- KAM template for listed entities; prior-year KAMs; EoM/OM precedent reports.

## Knowledge
- SA 700: Unmodified opinion when sufficient evidence + immaterial uncorrected + no scope limitation.
- SA 705.7/.8/.9: Qualified (material not pervasive) / Adverse (material and pervasive misstatement) / Disclaimer (material and pervasive scope limitation).
- SA 706.5/.9: EoM (matter appropriately disclosed; emphasis useful) / OM (matter other than FS relevant); neither modifies opinion; placed after opinion, before KAM.
- SA 701: KAM mandatory for listed entities — selected from significant risks and significant judgments.
- SA 710: Comparative — consistent with prior period; if restated, disclose basis.

## Workflow
1. Aggregate audit conclusions — summarise misstatements, going concern, subsequent events, fraud, related parties, internal controls.
2. Determine opinion type — IF sufficient evidence + immaterial uncorrected + no scope limitation THEN unmodified (SA 700); IF material but not pervasive THEN qualified (SA 705.7); IF material and pervasive misstatement THEN adverse (SA 705.8); IF material and pervasive scope limitation THEN disclaimer (SA 705.9).
3. Draft audit report per Sec 143(2)/(3) and SA 700 — title; addressee; opinion section; basis for opinion; KAM (listed); responsibilities; signature; date; place.
4. Add Basis for Modification paragraph (SA 705) — IF modified THEN describe matter(s); quantitative effect if quantifiable.
5. Add Emphasis of Matter (SA 706.5) — IF matter appropriately disclosed but emphasis useful THEN EoM (e.g., going concern material uncertainty, major litigation); Add Other Matter (SA 706.9) — IF matter other than FS relevant THEN OM (e.g., prior-year audit by different auditor).
6. Include KAM (SA 701 — listed entities) — select from significant risks and significant judgments; describe why significant and how addressed.
7. Verify comparative information (SA 710) — IF consistent THEN proceed; IF restated THEN verify restatement basis and disclosure.
8. Read other information (SA 720) — read Director's Report, CSR Report; IF material inconsistency THEN request correction; if refused → revise report / withdraw.
9. Add CARO 2020 reporting (Annexure) — report 16 clauses (i to xvi) based on audit evidence; IF exception → report clause-wise.
10. Address Sec 143(3) matters — carrying value of assets; loans and advances; dividend; subsidiary financials; internal financial controls; fraud (Sec 143(12)).
11. Coordinate joint auditors (Sec 139) — divide reporting responsibilities; sign jointly or separately with basis if disagreement.
12. Sign and date audit report — sign by engagement partner + firm (ICAI registration); date = authorization date; issue report; coordinate AOC-4 filing within 30 days of AGM.

## Decision Points
- D1: Sufficient appropriate evidence obtained? YES → proceed. NO → scope limitation (SA 705).
- D2: Aggregate uncorrected misstatements material? YES → assess pervasiveness. NO → unmodified.
- D3: Misstatement material and pervasive? YES → adverse opinion (SA 705.8). NO → qualified (SA 705.7).
- D4: Scope limitation material and pervasive? YES → disclaimer (SA 705.9). NO → qualified (SA 705.7).
- D5: Going concern material uncertainty adequately disclosed? YES → EoM paragraph (SA 706). NO → qualified/adverse.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Does audit evidence genuinely support the opinion, or are gaps dismissed?
- Are KAMs selected from significant risks or generic boilerplate?
- Are EoM paragraphs used to draw attention to genuine concerns or mask modifications?
- Is comparative information genuinely consistent or restated without disclosure?
- Is other information read in full or selectively?

## Considerations
- KAM mandatory for listed; not a substitute for modification.
- EoM: does not modify opinion; placed after Opinion and Basis; before KAM.
- OM: placed after Opinion, Basis, EoM, KAM.
- Comparative: if prior period unaudited → disclose; if audited by predecessor → OM paragraph.
- Joint auditors (Sec 139): sign jointly; if disagreement → sign separately with basis (SA 700.A60).

## Common Risks
- Inappropriate opinion type (qualified instead of adverse; unmodified instead of qualified).
- KAM generic boilerplate (not from significant risks).
- EoM used to mask going concern modification.
- Other information not read in full.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Opinion modified but basis understated to minimise market impact.
- KAMs selected to avoid disclosing sensitive significant risks.
- Comparative figures restated without disclosure to mask prior-period fraud.
- Other information (Director's Report) contradicts FS; auditor ignores.
- Joint auditor disagreement on fraud suppressed by dominant auditor.

## Common Pitfalls
- Using prior-year report template without updating for current findings.
- Confusing qualified vs adverse vs disclaimer criteria.
- Generic KAMs not tied to significant risks.
- Not reading other information (Director's Report, CSR Report).
- Not reconciling joint auditor reports.

## Validation
- [ ] Opinion type determined based on misstatement / scope / going concern assessment.
- [ ] Report drafted per SA 700 / 705 / 706; Basis for Opinion included.
- [ ] KAM included for listed entities (SA 701); EoM/OM added where applicable.
- [ ] Comparative information verified (SA 710); restatement basis disclosed if any.
- [ ] Other information read (SA 720); material inconsistency addressed.

## Expected Outputs
- Final auditor's report per SA 700/705/706/701/710/720 and Sec 143.
- Basis for modification paragraph (if modified).
- KAM section (listed entities); EoM / OM paragraphs (where applicable).
- CARO 2020 Annexure.

## Failure Conditions
- Audit procedures not substantially complete.
- Management representation letter not obtained.
- Material misstatements unresolved without opinion modification.

## Escalation Conditions
- Material pervasive misstatement / scope limitation → STOP, adverse / disclaimer opinion (SA 705), partner consultation.
- Going concern material uncertainty undisclosed → STOP, qualified/adverse opinion, partner consultation.
- Material fraud identified (Sec 143(12) ≥₹1 cr) → STOP, Form ADT-4 reporting, partner consultation.
- Joint auditor unresolved disagreement on material matter → STOP, partner consultation, separate report with basis.
- Other information materially inconsistent and management refuses correction → STOP, modify report / withdraw, partner consultation.
