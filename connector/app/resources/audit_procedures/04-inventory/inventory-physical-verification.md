# Inventory Physical Verification

## Purpose
Attend physical inventory count per SA 501. Evaluate count procedures, observe execution, perform test counts, and assess slow-moving/obsolete stock. CARO 2020 Cl. (xi) reporting on physical verification.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Material inventory at year-end; significant risk of inventory misstatement.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Inventory immaterial; perpetual inventory with continuous count and immaterial variance.
- AUDIT AREAS: Raw materials, WIP, finished goods, stores, stock at third parties, consignment.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Existence, completeness, accuracy, valuation (limited — valuation separate skill).
- Framework: SA 501, SA 500, SA 530, Ind AS 2, CARO 2020 Cl. (xi), Sec 143(3)(i).

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Risk Assessment, Internal Control Understanding, Materiality, Sampling.
- FEEDS: Inventory Valuation Testing, Inventory Cut-Off Testing, Evaluation of Misstatements.

## Required Inputs
- Inventory count instructions / SOP.
- Stock summary at year-end (by location, category).
- Count sheets / bar-code scanner output.
- Prior-year count exceptions and adjustments.
- List of inventory locations including third-party/consignment.

## Optional Inputs
- Continuous/perpetual inventory system records.
- Third-party confirmations (C&F agents, warehouses).
- Prior-year obsolete/slow-moving write-down schedule.

## Knowledge
- SA 501: Attend physical count if material; observe procedures, perform test counts.
- Ind AS 2: Inventory at lower of cost and NRV; cost formula (FIFO/weighted average).
- CARO 2020 Cl. (xi): Comment on physical verification coverage, discrepancies, treatment.
- Sec 143(3)(i): ICFR — controls over inventory.
- SA 505: Third-party stock confirmation.
- SA 530: Test count sample selection.

## Workflow
1. Obtain count instructions — review for adequacy:
   - Count teams, segregation, count sheets, reconciliation process.
   - IF instructions inadequate → request revision; do not rely.
2. Pre-count planning:
   - Identify all inventory locations (own, third-party, consignment in/out).
   - Determine attendance locations based on materiality and risk.
   - IF third-party location material → request SA 505 confirmation.
3. Attend count:
   - Observe count procedures — team composition, blind counts, second counts.
   - Verify segregation of counting team from custody team.
   - IF segregation absent → control deficiency.
4. Perform test counts:
   - Select items from floor to count sheet (completeness).
   - Select items from count sheet to floor (existence).
   - Document test count, sign count sheet.
   - IF variance > trivial → second count, investigate.
5. Verify cut-off information:
   - Last GRN number pre-count.
   - First dispatch number post-count.
   - Goods in transit at count.
6. Verify slow-moving/obsolete:
   - Identify aged inventory, damaged, obsolete items.
   - IF slow-moving > 180 days → flag for NRV write-down.
   - IF obsolete → write-down to net realisable value.
7. Verify stock at third parties:
   - C&F agents, consignment outward, warehouse.
   - Send SA 505 confirmations.
   - IF non-response → alternative procedures (stock statement from agent).
8. Verify consignment inward:
   - Stock with company on consignment — verify not included in own inventory.
9. Verify WIP:
   - Stage of completion — verify basis (cost-to-cost, output method).
   - IF stage not supported → valuation risk.
10. Verify stores and spares:
    - Identify capital spares vs consumables.
    - Capital spares → part of FA; consumables → inventory.
11. Reconcile count results to GL:
    - Count adjustments — investigate material variances.
    - IF adjustment > 5% → control deficiency, escalate.
12. Evaluate CARO 2020 Cl. (xi):
    - Coverage of physical verification.
    - Material discrepancies and treatment.
13. Conclude on inventory existence — document attendance, test counts, exceptions.

## Decision Points
- D1: Count instructions adequate? YES → proceed. NO → request revision, do not rely.
- D2: Segregation of counting team? YES → proceed. NO → control deficiency.
- D3: Test count variance > trivial? YES → second count, investigate. NO → accept.
- D4: Third-party stock material? YES → SA 505 confirmation. NO → standard.
- D5: Slow-moving/obsolete identified? YES → NRV write-down per Ind AS 2. NO → standard.
- D6: Count adjustment > 5%? YES → control deficiency, escalate. NO → document.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are count teams genuinely independent, or instructed by management?
- Are test counts pre-selected by management — concealment risk?
- Are slow-moving items genuinely usable, or written up?
- Are third-party stocks real, or fabricated confirmations?
- Are WIP stage estimates realistic, or profit-inflated?
- Are count variances explained, or accepted?
- Is obsolete stock segregated for write-down, or mixed with usable?

## Considerations
- Multiple locations: Stratify attendance by materiality and risk.
- Continuous count: Evaluate perpetual inventory system + ITGCs.
- Third-party: SA 505 confirmation; non-response → alternative procedures.
- WIP: Stage of completion — engineering certification may be needed.
- Capital spares: Ind AS 16 — part of FA, not inventory.
- Seasonal: Plan attendance based on seasonal peak/trough.

## Common Risks
- Count teams instructed by management — concealment.
- Test counts pre-selected — non-representative.
- Slow-moving/obsolete not written down — overvaluation.
- Third-party stock not confirmed — existence risk.
- WIP stage inflated — profit manipulation.
- Count variances not investigated — control weakness.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Count teams pre-instructed on what to count.
- Test count items pre-selected by management.
- Round-amount inventory balances.
- Slow-moving stock written up without justification.
- Third-party confirmations received via management.
- WIP stage significantly higher than prior year without rationale.

## Common Pitfalls
- Not attending all material locations.
- Performing floor-to-sheet only (missing existence direction).
- Skipping slow-moving/obsolete assessment.
- Not confirming third-party stock.
- Accepting count variances without investigation.
- Not verifying WIP stage independently.

## Validation
- [ ] Count instructions reviewed for adequacy.
- [ ] All material locations attended.
- [ ] Test counts performed (both directions).
- [ ] Cut-off information obtained (last GRN, first dispatch).
- [ ] Slow-moving/obsolete identified for NRV write-down.
- [ ] Third-party stock confirmed per SA 505.
- [ ] WIP stage of completion verified.
- [ ] Count results reconciled to GL.
- [ ] CARO 2020 Cl. (xi) evaluated.

## Expected Outputs
- Count attendance memo with observations.
- Test count schedule (both directions).
- Cut-off information log.
- Slow-moving/obsolete inventory schedule.
- Third-party stock confirmation summary.
- WIP stage verification memo.
- Count reconciliation to GL.
- CARO 2020 Cl. (xi) evaluation memo.
- Conclusion on inventory existence.

## Failure Conditions
- Count instructions inadequate.
- Management refuses attendance at material location.
- Third-party stock not confirmable and material.

## Escalation Conditions
- Count teams instructed by management → STOP, control deficiency, consider qualification.
- Test count variance material → STOP, second count, partner consultation.
- Slow-moving/obsolete material not written down → STOP, propose adjustment.
- Third-party stock confirmation not obtainable material → STOP, scope limitation, consider qualification.
- Count adjustment > 5% → STOP, escalate, control deficiency reporting.
