# Engagement Acceptance and Continuance

## Purpose
Evaluate whether to accept or continue a statutory audit engagement per SA 210 and Companies Act 2013. Safeguard independence, integrity, and competence before signing engagement letter.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: New client acceptance, recurring client continuance, partner rotation triggered, scope change.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Limited review or agreed-upon procedures engagement (use separate SRE/ISAE 3402 workflow).
- AUDIT AREAS: Independence, integrity, competence, preconditions, eligibility.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Engagement eligibility, independence compliance, precondition satisfaction.
- Framework: SA 210, SA 220, SQM 1, SQM 2; Sec 139, 140, 141, 144 Companies Act 2013; ICAI Code of Ethics.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: None (entry-point skill).
- FEEDS: Audit Planning and Strategy, Risk Assessment, Engagement Quality Review.

## Required Inputs
- Client name, CIN, registered office, list of group entities.
- Prior year audited financials (if recurring) and audit report including CARO 2020.
- Management integrity questionnaire, board composition, KMP list.
- Engagement partner and team CVs, independence declarations.
- Fee proposal and terms of engagement.

## Optional Inputs
- Prior auditor communication (mandatory on change under Sec 140).
- Internal audit reports, secretarial audit report, GST/IT returns.
- Group auditor instructions (if component).

## Knowledge
- SA 210.6: Preconditions — acceptable framework, management agreement to scope, adequate internal records.
- Sec 139: Auditor appointment at AGM; Sec 140(4): Filing ADT-3 within 30 days of resignation; communicate to previous auditor.
- Sec 141: Disqualifications — indebtedness, security holding, business relationship, subsidiary relationship.
- Sec 144: Prohibited non-audit services — bookkeeping, IS design, actuarial, investment banking, HR management.
- ICAI Code: Independence threats — self-review, self-interest, advocacy, familiarity, intimidation.
- SA 220: Engagement partner responsibility; EQCR for listed/PSP engagements.
- Rule 9 Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules 2014 — eligibility; NFRA for prescribed class.

## Workflow
1. Verify auditor eligibility under Sec 141 — check indebtedness, holding, relationship, convictions.
   - IF any disqualification THEN decline engagement.
2. Confirm appointment procedure — AGM resolution, ADT-1 filed within 15 days.
   - IF casual vacancy THEN verify Board action and member ratification within 3 months.
3. Communicate with previous auditor (mandatory on change under Sec 140).
   - IF no reply within 7 days OR objection raised THEN escalate; do not accept without resolution.
4. Evaluate management integrity — review prior year audit issues, regulatory actions, fraud history.
   - IF integrity concerns THEN decline; document reasons in client acceptance file.
5. Assess independence threats — self-review, familiarity, advocacy, self-interest, intimidation.
   - IF prohibited services under Sec 144 provided THEN cease or decline audit.
6. Verify preconditions per SA 210.6:
   - IF framework not acceptable (e.g., non-Ind AS for listed Co.) THEN decline.
   - IF management refuses responsibility for internal controls THEN decline.
   - IF records inadequate THEN decline or qualify scope.
7. Confirm engagement letter terms — scope, responsibilities, reporting format, fee, deadline.
8. Assess competence — sector expertise, IT systems knowledge, group audit experience.
   - IF competence gap THEN engage expert under SA 620 or decline.
9. For continuance: re-evaluate fees >15% of total (listCo 10%) — fees dependence threat.
   - IF fees exceed limit THEN additional safeguards or decline.
10. Document acceptance/continuance conclusion, partner sign-off, EQCR review for listed/PSP.
11. Issue engagement letter; obtain management signature before fieldwork.

## Decision Points
- D1: Any Sec 141 disqualification? YES → decline. NO → proceed.
- D2: Management integrity concerns? YES → decline. NO → proceed.
- D3: Independence threats manageable with safeguards? YES → apply safeguards, proceed. NO → decline.
- D4: Preconditions per SA 210 satisfied? YES → proceed. NO → decline or qualify.
- D5: Listed/PSP — EQCR support obtained? YES → proceed. NO → obtain before signing.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Has prior auditor raised integrity or qualification concerns?
- Any unexplained change in auditor — management pressure?
- Are fees unusually low — quality risk or fees dependence?
- Any related-party transactions or complex structures masking fraud?
- Does management resist providing information or scope limitations?
- Any pending litigation or regulatory action affecting integrity?
- Does KMP have history of association with failed companies?

## Considerations
- Group audit: Accept component only after communication with group auditor.
- Listed Co.: Stricter independence, mandatory EQCR, audit committee approval per Sec 177.
- PSUs: CAG appointment norms; follow CAG directions.
- First-year audit: Opening balances (SA 510), prior period comparatives.
- Rotation: Sec 139(2) for listed/large Co.; check 5/10-year cycle compliance.

## Common Risks
- Accepting client with prior auditor unresolved issues — professional liability.
- Overlooking Sec 144 prohibited services — disqualification + penalty.
- Fees dependence compromising independence — SA 220 breach.
- Inadequate competence for complex sector (banking, NBFC) — audit failure.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Frequent auditor changes with weak rationale — management shopping.
- Management reluctance on prior auditor communication — concealment.
- History of qualified/adverse opinions — recurring issues.
- Pending fraud/investigation cases against KMP or company.
- Significant RPTs without Sec 188 approval — governance concerns.
- Unexplained accounting policy changes with auditor turnover.

## Common Pitfalls
- Skipping prior auditor communication — professional misconduct.
- Accepting without independence evaluation — SA 220 breach.
- Engagement letter signed after fieldwork start — documentation failure.
- Not evaluating fees dependence for non-listed also — oversight.
- Group component acceptance without group auditor dialogue — scope gap.

## Validation
- [ ] Sec 141 eligibility verified — nil disqualification.
- [ ] ADT-1 filing confirmed (or ADT-3 in case of resignation).
- [ ] Prior auditor communication documented.
- [ ] Independence evaluation signed by partner.
- [ ] Preconditions SA 210 confirmed by management.
- [ ] Engagement letter executed before fieldwork.
- [ ] EQCR sign-off for listed/PSP.

## Expected Outputs
- Client acceptance/continuance evaluation memo.
- Independence declaration by engagement team.
- Prior auditor communication record.
- Signed engagement letter.
- Competence assessment and expert engagement plan (if any).
- Partner approval and EQCR sign-off (listed/PSP).

## Failure Conditions
- Sec 141 disqualification identified.
- Management refuses prior auditor communication.
- Preconditions not satisfied.
- Independence threat cannot be reduced to acceptable level.

## Escalation Conditions
- Prior auditor raises unresolved fraud/integrity concern → STOP, escalate to Risk Management Partner.
- Disqualification discovered post-acceptance → STOP, resign per Sec 140, file ADT-3.
- Independence breach identified during engagement → STOP, escalate to EQCR, consider withdrawal.
- Management integrity issue surfaces → STOP, escalate to Risk Management Partner, consider resignation.
