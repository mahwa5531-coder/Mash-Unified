# Materiality Determination

## Purpose
Compute overall materiality (OM), performance materiality (PM), and specific materiality (SM) per SA 320. Establish threshold for uncorrected misstatements per SA 450. Revise during audit as needed.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every statutory audit at planning; revised if conditions change.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Compilation, agreed-upon procedures, or non-audit engagements.
- AUDIT AREAS: FS-level thresholds, account-level thresholds, disclosure-level thresholds.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Materiality benchmark reflects user needs; thresholds applied consistently.
- Framework: SA 320, SA 450, ICAI Technical Guide on Materiality; Ind AS 1 (materiality in disclosure).

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Engagement Acceptance, Understanding Entity, Trial Balance Validation.
- FEEDS: Audit Planning, Risk Assessment, all execution skills, Evaluation of Misstatements, Reporting.

## Required Inputs
- Audited prior-year financials (PBT, revenue, total assets, net worth).
- Current-year draft financials / management projections.
- Industry benchmarks and peer ratios.
- Entity type (listed, SME, PSU, NBFC, insurance).

## Optional Inputs
- Analyst expectations, share price volatility (for listed).
- Regulatory capital thresholds (RBI, IRDAI).
- Loan covenant ratios.

## Knowledge
- SA 320.5: OM based on judgement; benchmark sensitive to user needs.
- SA 320.9: PM = OM × (50-75%) — lower % for higher risk/history of errors.
- SA 450: Cumulative uncorrected misstatements vs PM — assess impact on FS.
- Ind AS 1.7: Materiality applies to disclosures, not just line items.
- Companies Act Sec 143(3): Materiality affects qualification language.
- Listed Co.: Threshold also considers SEBI LODR materiality for events.
- Benchmarks (typical): PBT 5%, Revenue 0.5-1%, Total Assets 1-2%, Net Worth 1-2%, Equity 1%.

## Workflow
1. Select benchmark based on entity profile:
   - IF profit-oriented, stable PBT → use PBT @ 5%.
   - IF loss-making or volatile PBT → use Revenue @ 0.5-1% or Total Assets @ 1-2%.
   - IF holding/investment Co. → use Net Worth @ 1-2%.
   - IF NBFC/financial services → use Total Assets @ 0.5-1% or Net Worth @ 1-2%.
2. Compute OM = benchmark × chosen %.
   - IF OM abnormal vs prior year or peers → re-evaluate benchmark.
3. Determine PM:
   - Default PM = OM × 60%.
   - IF high risk or history of errors → PM = OM × 50%.
   - IF low risk, stable operations → PM = OM × 75%.
4. Determine SM for specific items:
   - RPT revenue/expenses → SM lower (e.g., 0.25% of revenue).
   - Director remuneration → SM = ₹1 lakh (Sec 198/17 compliance).
   - CSR expenditure → SM = 0.5% of net profit (Sec 135).
   - ESOP/deferred tax → SM aligned with relevant Ind AS impact.
5. Determine trivial threshold for accumulating misstatements (typically PM × 1-5%).
6. Document rationale for benchmark, %, PM factor — partner sign-off.
7. Apply materiality to:
   - Sample sizes (lower materiality → larger sample).
   - Disclosures (Ind AS 1 materiality).
   - Subsequent events evaluation.
8. Revise materiality if:
   - Final financials materially differ from draft.
   - New significant risk identified.
   - Material business change (M&A, impairment).
   - IF revised → re-evaluate procedures performed; extend if necessary.
9. Communicate materiality to component auditor (group audits).
10. Document final materiality, performance materiality, and specific materiality in strategy memo.

## Decision Points
- D1: PBT stable and positive? YES → use PBT @ 5%. NO → use Revenue/Assets/Net Worth.
- D2: High audit risk? YES → PM = OM × 50%. NO → PM = OM × 60-75%.
- D3: RPT/CSR/director remuneration material? YES → set SM. NO → rely on OM/PM.
- D4: Material conditions changed mid-audit? YES → revise materiality, re-evaluate. NO → continue.
- D5: Component auditor involved? YES → set component materiality ≤ group PM. NO → apply entity-level.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Is the benchmark chosen to mask volatility or loss?
- Is PM factor justified by risk, or set high to reduce work?
- Are specific materialities set for sensitive accounts (RPT, CSR, ESOP)?
- Has prior-year materiality been reviewed for appropriateness in current year?
- Has management's choice of accounting policy affected benchmark?

## Considerations
- Qualitative materiality: fraud, illegal acts, RPT non-disclosure — material regardless of amount.
- Listed Co.: Market capitalisation also relevant for KAM identification.
- First-year audit: Compare with predecessor auditor materiality; document differences.
- Group audit: Component materiality must allow aggregation to group PM.
- Disclosures: Materiality applies per Ind AS 1 — small items can be material qualitatively.
- Comparative: Use same materiality for current and prior period for consistency.

## Common Risks
- Using PBT when entity has volatile/management-adjusted PBT — masks errors.
- Setting PM too high → reduced procedures, missed errors.
- Not revising materiality when financials change post-planning.
- Ignoring qualitative materiality for RPT/fraud.
- Component materiality exceeding group PM — aggregation gap.
- Using same benchmark across unrelated group entities.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Benchmark changed mid-year without justification.
- PM set unusually high to avoid extended procedures.
- Management pushing for lower materiality on RPT.
- Disclosure materiality ignored for related-party balances.
- Comparative period materiality changed to mask prior-year errors.

## Common Pitfalls
- Defaulting to PBT without evaluating entity profile.
- PM factor not documented with risk rationale.
- Specific materiality omitted for CSR, ESOP, RPT.
- Not revisiting materiality when management accounts change.
- Failing to communicate materiality to component auditor.
- Using materiality as a "writing-off" threshold instead of accumulation threshold.

## Validation
- [ ] Benchmark selection justified in writing.
- [ ] OM, PM, SM computed and partner-approved.
- [ ] PM factor risk-justified.
- [ ] Specific materiality for RPT/CSR/director remuneration.
- [ ] Revision trigger criteria documented.
- [ ] Component materiality communicated (group audits).
- [ ] Trivial threshold for accumulating misstatements set.

## Expected Outputs
- Materiality computation memo (signed by partner).
- Specific materiality schedule for sensitive accounts.
- Component materiality instructions (if group).
- Materiality revision log (if any).

## Failure Conditions
- Benchmark cannot be justified based on entity profile.
- Materiality exceeds acceptable thresholds for listed entity.
- Partner does not sign off on materiality.

## Escalation Conditions
- Materiality revision reduces procedures post-completion → STOP, re-perform procedures.
- PM factor set below 50% or above 75% → escalate to partner for justification.
- Component materiality inconsistent with group PM → STOP, escalate to group engagement partner.
- Benchmark manipulated to mask loss → STOP, escalate, consider qualification.
