# Related Party Transactions Testing

## Purpose
Test identification, measurement, disclosure, and Sec 188 compliance of related party transactions per Ind AS 24 and SA 550.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every audit; RPT present (Sec 2(76) of Companies Act 2013).
- DO NOT USE WHEN: No RPT identified (rare — verify before excluding).
- AUDIT AREAS: RPT identification, arm's length price (ALP), Sec 188 compliance, balances, disclosures, KMP compensation.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Completeness, occurrence, valuation, presentation/disclosure.
- Framework: Ind AS 24; SA 550; Companies Act Sec 2(76), 188, 134(3)(h); CARO 2020 Cl. (xvii)/(xx); Rule 15.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Related Party Identification, Journal Entry Testing, Revenue Testing, Purchase Testing, Borrowings Testing.
- FEEDS: Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting, CARO 2020 Reporting.

## Required Inputs
- Management list of related parties (Sec 2(76)); RPT register.
- MGT-7 (annual return); board minutes; shareholders' register; KMP compensation schedule.
- Contracts with related parties; ALP workings; Sec 188 omnibus approval details.
- Prior-year related party working paper; confirmation of balances.

## Optional Inputs
- Transfer pricing study; industry benchmarks for similar transactions.

## Knowledge
- Sec 2(76): Related party — directors' relatives, KMP, firms with director/partner, subsidiary/holding/associate, JV.
- Sec 188: Specific RPTs require board / members' approval (sale/purchase of goods >10% turnover or ₹100 cr; property; leasing; borrowing/guarantees >10% net worth or ₹100 cr).
- Rule 15: Omnibus approval for recurring transactions — one year; audit committee ratification.
- Sec 134(3)(h): Disclose RPT in boards' report with justification.
- Ind AS 24.18: Disclose relationships, transactions, balances, commitments by category; KMP compensation.

## Workflow
1. Obtain management RPT list; reconcile with Related Party Identification skill output — IF list incomplete THEN obtain additional list and document representation.
2. Verify RPT identification per Sec 2(76) — cross-check MGT-7; board minutes; KMP compensation; prior-year working papers.
3. Test contracts / transactions — obtain contracts; verify terms; tie to books; IF no contract for material RPT THEN exception.
4. Test ALP — compare with comparable uncontrolled transactions; IF price materially different THEN assess justification; IF no comparable THEN challenge management's basis.
5. Test Sec 188 compliance — for Sec 188 transactions, verify board/members approval; audit committee omnibus approval (Rule 15); IF approval absent THEN reportable under Sec 188(5).
6. Test KMP compensation — verify salary, perquisites, commission (Sec 197 limits); sitting fees for independent directors; ESOP valuation.
7. Test RPT balances — send confirmations (SA 505); assess recoverability; IF long-outstanding THEN provision.
8. Test guarantees / loans to related parties (Sec 185/186) — verify Sec 185 prohibition (loans to directors) with exception for holding to subsidiary; Sec 186 limits.
9. Test related party revenue / purchases — verify substance; IF circular flow suspected THEN challenge; verify cut-off.
10. Test downstream / upstream flows — identify unrealised profit in inventory / fixed assets; eliminate per Ind AS 110.
11. Test disclosure (Ind AS 24.18 + Sec 134(3)(h)) — verify category-wise disclosure; names; ALP justification; KMP compensation; commitments.
12. Test CARO 2020 Cl. (xvii)/(xx) — verify ordinary course and ALP basis; report separately if not. Aggregate misstatements per SA 450.

## Decision Points
- D1: RPT list reconciles with identification skill? YES → proceed. NO → obtain additional list.
- D2: Transaction in ordinary course at ALP? YES → proceed. NO → Sec 188 approval; report if absent.
- D3: Sec 188 approval obtained? YES → proceed. NO → reportable; assess materiality.
- D4: Receivable from related party recoverable? YES → proceed. NO → provision.
- D5: Sec 185 (loan to director) applicable? YES → prohibited (except holding to wholly-owned subsidiary). NO → proceed under Sec 186.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are transactions genuinely at arm's length or window-dressed to comply?
- Are RPTs routed through shell companies to disguise relationship?
- Is KMP compensation aligned with disclosed terms or include undisclosed perquisites?
- Are receivables from related parties recoverable or quasi-equity?
- Are RPTs in ordinary course or disguised one-off transactions?

## Considerations
- Ind AS 24 disclosure applies even if nil outstanding for the period.
- ALP basis: CUP, resale price, cost-plus, TNMM, profit split.
- Sec 188 materiality thresholds (10% turnover or ₹100 cr; 10% net worth or ₹100 cr) — check both.
- Sec 185: Prohibition on loan/guarantee/security to directors or their relatives.
- Joint venture / associate: significant influence = 20%+ voting power generally.

## Common Risks
- RPT not in management list (undisclosed).
- ALP not genuinely tested; management certification only.
- Sec 188 approvals missing or omnibus approval exceeded.
- KMP compensation under-disclosed (perquisites, ESOP, commission).
- Related party receivables not provided for despite age/uncollectibility.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Undisclosed RPT identified in board minutes or shareholders' register.
- Round-tripping through related party to inflate revenue.
- Related party transactions at non-arm's length to siphon funds.
- KMP compensation undisclosed to avoid Sec 197 limits.
- Loans to directors disguised as advances to vendors (Sec 185 breach).

## Common Pitfalls
- Accepting management RPT list without cross-checking MGT-7 / board minutes.
- Not testing ALP with comparable data.
- Missing Sec 188 materiality thresholds (turnover vs net worth).
- Not confirming related party balances.
- Treating inter-company receivables as recoverable without ageing analysis.

## Validation
- [ ] Management RPT list reconciled with Related Party Identification skill.
- [ ] Each material RPT tested for contract, terms, ALP.
- [ ] Sec 188 / Rule 15 approvals verified; Sec 185/186 compliance tested.
- [ ] KMP compensation per Sec 197 verified and disclosed.
- [ ] RPT balances confirmed (SA 505); recoverability assessed.

## Expected Outputs
- RPT testing master working paper with category-wise summary.
- ALP compliance memo for material RPT.
- Sec 188 / Rule 15 / Sec 185-186 compliance memo.
- KMP compensation memo.

## Failure Conditions
- Management RPT list materially incomplete; refuses to update.
- Contracts not produced for material RPT.
- ALP basis not documented for material RPT.
- Sec 188 approval absent for material RPT.

## Escalation Conditions
- Undisclosed RPT material → STOP, SA 550 + SA 240, partner consultation.
- Sec 185 breach (loan to director) material → STOP, Sec 143(12) reporting.
- RPT fraud (round-tripping, siphoning) → STOP, SA 240, partner consultation.
- ALP materially deviated without justification → STOP, modify opinion if disclosure inadequate.
