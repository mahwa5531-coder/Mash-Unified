# Going Concern Assessment

## Purpose
Evaluate management's going concern assessment and assess material uncertainty disclosure per SA 570.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every audit — basic assessment; in-depth when indicators present.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Liquidation basis already adopted by entity (use SA 700 with modified framework).
- AUDIT AREAS: Net worth, recurring losses, working capital, loan defaults, going concern disclosure, management plans.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Going concern basis appropriateness, valuation, presentation/disclosure.
- Framework: SA 570; Ind AS 1; Sec 143(1); CARO 2020 Cl. (xxiii).

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Borrowings Testing, Cash & Bank Testing, JE Testing, Subsequent Events Review, Financial Statement Analysis.
- FEEDS: Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting (SA 705 EoM / Disclaimer).

## Required Inputs
- Management going concern assessment memo (12-month forecast cash flow).
- Cash flow forecast with assumptions; sensitivity analysis.
- Loan agreements with covenants; bank correspondences; RBI / NPA classification.
- Board minutes (post year-end); management plans (cost reduction, asset sale, equity infusion).

## Optional Inputs
- Independent expert valuation; credit rating reports; industry outlook.

## Knowledge
- SA 570.11: Evaluate management's assessment process; appropriateness of 12-month period from reporting date.
- SA 570.13: Period not less than 12 months from reporting date (not authorization date).
- SA 570.16: Material uncertainty exists but adequate disclosure → EoM paragraph.
- SA 570.17: Material uncertainty exists but inadequate disclosure → qualified/adverse opinion.
- SA 570.19: Use of going concern basis inappropriate → adverse opinion.

## Workflow
1. Identify going concern indicators — negative net worth; recurring losses; working capital deficiency; loan default; loss of major customer; supplier withdrawal; staff attrition; denial of credit.
2. Obtain management assessment memo and 12-month cash flow forecast — IF not prepared or inadequate THEN auditor prepares own assessment.
3. Evaluate forecast assumptions — test revenue growth vs industry; verify cost reduction commitments; assess asset sale feasibility; equity infusion from bank statements / board resolution / investor agreement.
4. Test loan defaults and covenants — obtain all loan agreements; verify compliance; IF breach THEN assess bank waiver / RBI NPA classification.
5. Test working capital — compute current ratio; IF <1 (or deteriorating) THEN assess; verify inventory ageing and receivables recoverability.
6. Test post year-end events (link to Subsequent Events Review) — IF loan recall / supplier withdrawal / lost customer THEN reassess going concern.
7. Evaluate management plans — cost reduction: verify specifics (headcount, facilities) with timeline and board approval; asset sale: verify binding agreement; IF not binding THEN no reliance; equity infusion: verify from bank credit advice post year-end / binding investor agreement; refinancing: verify from bank sanction letter.
8. Sensitivity analysis — test ±5-10% revenue / cost / timing; IF small change reverses conclusion THEN material uncertainty exists.
9. Assess going concern conclusion:
   - IF going concern appropriate, no material uncertainty → unmodified opinion.
   - IF going concern appropriate, material uncertainty adequately disclosed → EoM paragraph (SA 706).
   - IF going concern appropriate, material uncertainty not adequately disclosed → qualified/adverse (SA 705).
   - IF going concern inappropriate → adverse opinion (SA 705).
10. Verify disclosure per Ind AS 1.25-26 + CARO Cl. (xxiii) if applicable.
11. Document management representation on going concern (SA 580).
12. Communicate with those charged with governance per SA 260.

## Decision Points
- D1: Going concern indicators present? YES → in-depth assessment. NO → basic assessment only.
- D2: Management plans feasible (binding agreements / verifiable actions)? YES → reliance. NO → material uncertainty likely.
- D3: Material uncertainty exists? YES → assess disclosure. NO → unmodified opinion.
- D4: Material uncertainty adequately disclosed per Ind AS 1.25? YES → EoM paragraph. NO → qualified/adverse.
- D5: Going concern basis appropriate? YES → proceed. NO → adverse opinion.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are equity infusion commitments binding or just letters of intent?
- Is asset sale pricing realistic given market conditions?
- Are forecasts over-optimistic on revenue / cost reduction?
- Are loan covenant breaches fully disclosed or selectively?
- Is post year-end evidence consistent with year-end going concern assessment?

## Considerations
- 12-month period from reporting date (not authorization date) — SA 570.13.
- NPA classification under RBI norms — impact on bank lending and going concern.
- Material uncertainty disclosure: nature, events, conditions, management's response.
- EoM paragraph included even when adequately disclosed (SA 706 + SA 570.16).
- Adverse opinion if going concern basis inappropriate; disclaimer if scope limitation prevents assessment.

## Common Risks
- Material uncertainty not identified due to management optimism.
- Equity infusion plans relied upon without binding commitments.
- Loan covenant breaches not disclosed.
- Working capital deficiency understated through window-dressing.
- Forecasts not stress-tested with sensitivity.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Equity infusion announced but not backed by bank credit advice.
- Asset sale at inflated price to related party to support going concern.
- Loan covenant breaches concealed by window-dressing ratios.
- Revenue forecast inflated with no historical basis.
- Cost reduction plans vague to mask continued losses.

## Common Pitfalls
- Not preparing independent forecast when management assessment inadequate.
- Relying on non-binding LOIs for equity infusion.
- Missing sensitivity analysis on key assumptions.
- Omitting EoM paragraph despite adequate disclosure.
- Not assessing post year-end events impact on going concern.

## Validation
- [ ] Going concern indicators identified; management assessment obtained.
- [ ] 12-month forecast tested; assumptions verified; sensitivity performed.
- [ ] Loan covenant compliance tested; defaults assessed.
- [ ] Management plans verified (binding agreements / bank advices / board approval).
- [ ] Post year-end events reviewed (link to Subsequent Events skill).

## Expected Outputs
- Going concern assessment master working paper.
- Forecast evaluation memo with sensitivity analysis.
- Loan covenant compliance memo.
- Management plans evaluation memo (equity infusion, asset sale, refinancing, cost reduction).

## Failure Conditions
- Management assessment not prepared and refuses to do so.
- Cash flow forecast with no documented assumptions.
- Loan agreements not produced.

## Escalation Conditions
- Material uncertainty exists and management refuses to disclose → STOP, qualified/adverse opinion (SA 705), partner consultation.
- Going concern basis inappropriate and management insists → STOP, adverse opinion (SA 705.19), partner consultation.
- Equity infusion plan fraudulent (forged sanction) → STOP, SA 240, Sec 143(12) reporting.
- Scope limitation prevents assessment → STOP, disclaimer of opinion (SA 705), partner consultation.
