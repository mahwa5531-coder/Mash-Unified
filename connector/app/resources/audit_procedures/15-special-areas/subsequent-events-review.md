# Subsequent Events Review

## Purpose
Identify and assess events after the reporting period for adjusting or non-adjusting treatment per SA 560.

## Scope
- USE WHEN: Every audit; events between reporting date and audit report authorization date.
- DO NOT USE WHEN: Events after audit report authorization (discover subsequently — SA 560.14).
- AUDIT AREAS: Litigation, dividends, restructuring, impairment, going concern, cut-off, write-offs.

## Audit Objective
- Assertions: Completeness, valuation, presentation/disclosure.
- Framework: SA 560; Ind AS 10; Sec 143(1)/(2) of Companies Act.

## Workflow Dependencies
- REQUIRES: Litigation/Legal Review, Provisions Testing, Revenue Testing, Inventory Testing, Going Concern Assessment.
- FEEDS: Audit Conclusion, Audit Reporting, Management Representation Letter.

## Required Inputs
- Board meeting minutes post year-end till authorization date.
- Subsequent sales, purchases, payments, receipts register.
- Legal cases update; advocate letters post year-end.
- Management subsequent events memo; post year-end financial information.
- Bank statements; significant contracts post year-end; press releases.

## Optional Inputs
- Analyst calls transcript; press releases; competitor / industry news; subsequent financing.

## Knowledge
- SA 560.6: Adjusting events — conditions existing at reporting date; adjust FS.
- SA 560.7: Non-adjusting events — conditions arising after reporting date; disclose if material.
- SA 560.13: Procedures to identify events up to audit report date.
- SA 560.14: If facts become known after report date — Sec 143(1) re-issuance.
- Ind AS 10.21: Disclose date of authorization for issue and who authorized.

## Workflow
1. Obtain management subsequent events memo covering events till authorization date.
2. Review board minutes post year-end till audit report authorization date — IF dividend declared post year-end THEN disclose (Ind AS 10.12); do not recognise liability.
3. Review post year-end financial information — tie to interim management accounts; identify material variances; assess linkage to year-end balances.
4. Test adjusting events — IF customer bankruptcy post year-end THEN ECL provision at year-end (Ind AS 109); IF court order post year-end against year-end case THEN adjust provision (Ind AS 37); IF inventory NRV decline confirmed THEN write down at year-end; IF fraud discovered affecting year-end THEN adjust.
5. Test non-adjusting events — IF major acquisition / fire / natural disaster post year-end THEN disclose if material; IF share issue / buyback post year-end THEN disclose.
6. Test cut-off for sales / purchases / payments / receipts in first 1-2 months.
7. Test litigation update — obtain advocate letters post year-end; review for changes; IF order post year-end confirms estimate THEN adjust (SA 540/560).
8. Test restructuring events — IF announced post year-end but obligation existed at year-end THEN provision; IF arose post year-end THEN disclose.
9. Test going concern events — IF post year-end events cast significant doubt (lost major customer, loan recall) THEN reassess going concern (SA 570).
10. Test re-issuance — IF facts become known after authorization requiring FS revision THEN management revises; auditor re-issues report referencing SA 560.14.
11. Verify authorization date disclosure (Ind AS 10.21) and audit report dating (single date = authorization date; dual date for specific event).
12. Document management representation on subsequent events (SA 580). Aggregate misstatements and disclosures per SA 450.

## Decision Points
- D1: Condition existed at reporting date? YES → adjusting event (adjust FS). NO → non-adjusting (disclose if material).
- D2: Dividend declared post year-end? YES → disclose (not recognise). NO → no impact.
- D3: Post year-end customer bankruptcy material? YES → ECL adjustment at year-end. NO → no adjustment.
- D4: Court order post year-end against year-end case? YES → adjust provision. NO → disclose if material event.
- D5: Going concern material uncertainty triggered post year-end? YES → reassess (SA 570). NO → proceed.

## Professional Skepticism Probes
- Are subsequent sales genuinely post year-end or backdated?
- Are adverse events (fire, customer loss) suppressed in management memo?
- Is the authorization date genuinely the latest date of substantive audit work?
- Are board minutes complete or selected minutes provided?
- Is going concern evidence post year-end contradictory to year-end assessment?

## Considerations
- Authorization date per Ind AS 10.21 = date board approves FS for issue; auditor's report date same.
- Dual dating not common in India (Sec 143 single date).
- Post year-end sale of inventory below cost → potential NRV impairment at year-end.
- Post year-end litigation settlement significantly different from provision → estimate assessment (SA 540).
- Going concern: 12-month assessment from reporting date using all available information.

## Common Risks
- Adjusting events not recognised (customer bankruptcy, court orders).
- Non-adjusting material events not disclosed.
- Dividend post year-end wrongly recognised as liability.
- Going concern deterioration post year-end not assessed.
- Authorization date set too early to suppress events.

## Fraud Triggers (SA 240)
- Subsequent sales backdated to year-end to inflate revenue.
- Bad debts written off post year-end on year-end receivables (cookie jar).
- Customer bankruptcy post year-end on year-end receivable (ECL understated at year-end).
- Court orders post year-end suppressed to avoid provision adjustment.
- Authorization date manipulated to exclude adverse events.

## Common Pitfalls
- Not reviewing all board meetings between year-end and authorization date.
- Treating customer bankruptcy as non-adjusting instead of adjusting.
- Recognising dividend post year-end as liability.
- Not assessing going concern impact of post year-end events.
- Setting authorization date before completing substantive procedures.

## Validation
- [ ] Management subsequent events memo obtained.
- [ ] Board minutes reviewed from year-end to authorization date.
- [ ] Adjusting events identified and adjusted in FS.
- [ ] Non-adjusting material events disclosed (Ind AS 10).
- [ ] Cut-off testing performed for first 1-2 months.

## Expected Outputs
- Subsequent events review working paper with event classification.
- Adjusting events adjustment memo; non-adjusting events disclosure memo.
- Board minutes review memo (post year-end meetings).
- Cut-off testing memo (first 1-2 months).

## Failure Conditions
- Management unwilling to provide post year-end board minutes.
- Post year-end financial information not prepared.
- Authorization date not determinable.

## Escalation Conditions
- Material adjusting event not adjusted → STOP, require management adjustment or modify opinion.
- Going concern material uncertainty emerged post year-end → STOP, SA 570 reassessment, partner consultation.
- Authorization date manipulated to suppress events → STOP, partner consultation, integrity concern.
- Post year-end fraud discovered affecting year-end FS → STOP, SA 240, Sec 143(12) reporting.
